Findings

F-1HIGH

Cosmos Hub voting power concentrated

Top-10 validators hold 56.27% of bonded tokens; top-1 holds 18.69%. The top 10 alone exceed the 50% approval threshold and the 40% quorum.

F-2HIGH

Proposal #1056 decided below quorum

Proposal #1056 “ATOM Refund & Justice Bounty” decided with 29.90% turnout, below the 40% quorum; independently veto-rejected at 89.74% no-with-veto (91,513,508,906,190 of 101,976,738,214,747 uatom). Voting 2026-09-23 to 2026-09-30. Turnout uses bonded tokens at retrieval; for #1056 to have met quorum, vote-time bonded would have had to be at most 254,941,845,536,868 uatom, about 25% below the current total.

F-3MEDIUM

Ethereum ACD meeting notes moved out of the canonical repo directories

Latest EL note Meeting 204 (Jan 30, 2025), latest CL note call_150 (Feb 6, 2025); the live record is the README meeting table plus external transcripts.

F-4MEDIUM

Consensus client-diversity datasets disagree

MigaLabs (visible): Lighthouse 48.14%; Blockprint (hidden): Teku 99.83%; Rated (hidden): Teku 53.86%.

F-5MEDIUM

eips.ethereum.org/all lists 1,232 numbers, ethereum/EIPs master holds 972 files; 260 numbers are ERCs now in ethereum/ERCs (verified: ERC-838, ERC-7524, ERC-8444).

Geth holds 50.13% execution node share (ethernodes) — above a simple majority, below the 66.6% supermajority threshold.

03

How to reproduce

$python3 src/fetch_governance.py

Run: python3 src/fetch_governance.py (Python stdlib only, no API keys, ~2 minutes). It fetches live data, saves every raw retrieval under outputs/raw/ with timestamps and SHA-256, scores the 12 rules, and writes outputs/results.json and outputs/results.txt.
